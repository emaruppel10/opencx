Security, Governance & Data Ownership

Your rules aren’t an obstacle. They’re the starting point.

Most AI vendors ask for broad access to your systems and ask you to trust their platform’s controls. OpenCX works the other way. Tell us your constraints, and we build within them, not around them. If your compliance team won’t allow a direct connection to your ERP, that’s not a blocker, it’s a spec. We build from data you upload or export on your own terms, same intelligence, zero forced access. If your security team runs specific protocols, we operate inside them rather than asking you to adopt ours. IntiConnect, the layer that connects your systems, is a bridge, not a vault, it moves your data between systems without storing it. And everything built along the way, the data, the trained models, the IP, stays yours. Permanently.

Why Not Just Use a Traditional Enterprise Platform

Salesforce Agentforce, Genesys, NICE CXone, Microsoft Copilot Studio, these are capable platforms, and for some businesses they work. But they’re SaaS tools built as closed ecosystems: you rent access, you conform to their data model, their access requirements, their certification boundaries. Once you’re in, you’re in, and migrating out means rebuilding what you spent years configuring, because you never owned the system, you were only ever renting space inside it. OpenCX isn’t a tool you subscribe to. It’s a framework you own. We’re integration-agnostic and policy-agnostic, built around your governance instead of asking you to replace it. There’s no system integrator standing between you and your own data, and your information is never outsourced to a third party along the way. What gets built together stays yours.

Why Not Just Use Open Sourced LLM’s

A great model isn’t a great operation.

Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini are genuinely excellent at reasoning. That’s not in question. But a reasoning engine isn’t the same thing as a system that understands and runs your business. Those tools can summarize a document. They can’t automatically route a request based on your budget thresholds, score a candidate against your hiring criteria, flag a compliance risk specific to your industry, or trigger an action in your CRM without a human copying and pasting it there. That’s the gap OpenCX closes. We’re not reinventing the model underneath; we use the same class of foundation models everyone else does. What we built is the understanding layer above it: trained on your own data instead of the open internet, and the orchestration that turns that understanding into action. A great model is the engine. OpenCX is the vehicle, the cockpit, and the driver trained specifically for your roads.
