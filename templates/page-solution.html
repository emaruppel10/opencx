Workflow Automation

Stop losing hours to handoffs.

Problem

Somewhere in your business, there's a process that moves through five departments, three approvals, and at least one spreadsheet that only one person knows how to update. It works, technically. It also quietly costs you days every time it runs.

Solution

OpenCX starts by understanding that process, not just automating the steps someone wrote down. It maps how the work actually happens, then automates the handoffs. Requisitions route themselves based on the rules you set. Approvals trigger automatically. People only get pulled in when a human decision actually matters.

Business Impact
  • Multi-step processes that used to take days now move in hours
  • Fewer manual errors from copy-paste handoffs between systems
  • Your team spends time on judgment calls, not data entry
  • Full visibility into where every process actually stands
