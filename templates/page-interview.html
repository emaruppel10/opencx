Interview

Dimitrios Politis,
Owner and Operator, Nexx Burger

OpenCX:

What does a day running the operation of Nexx Burger actually look like for you?

Dimitrios:

We currently have three restaurants and one catering truck. What I do is oversee the operation, seeing how we can improve it, not just operations, but service and food too, the whole picture. How can we make customers come in and leave with a smile, and come back again? I like to see it from the customer's journey first, then work my way back to the business.

OpenCX:

And where are those locations?

Dimitrios:

We're based in California. Our Downey store is in Los Angeles County. In Orange County we have two stores, in the city of Orange and in Santa Ana. Our food truck is based out of Bell Gardens, right next to Downey, and it drives out to corporate events, birthday parties, weddings, wherever it's needed.

OpenCX:

So before OpenCX ever entered the picture, what was the actual moment that got you thinking about AI in the first place?

Dimitrios:

In the fast food industry, sometimes the volume and traffic get so high that we don't have time to pick up the phone. And that's a missed phone call, which means possibly missed revenue, whether that's a phone order or a catering order, which is usually a lot bigger. That's what made me start looking into AI, to see how I could improve the operation without losing revenue, while letting my team focus on the customers on site instead of someone having to leave the register to take a call.

OpenCX:

You mentioned the catering side specifically feels that pain the most. Walk me through what changed there.

Dimitrios:

We incorporated it into our catering truck. Now if you call us, the AI picks up, gives you all the information about the truck, requirements, and how to book. You can even ask if we're available on a specific date, and it checks the calendar and tells you. That process used to take five to ten minutes on the phone, and if we're busy, that's five to ten minutes. I've lost a team member who could be helping push out service.

OpenCX:

Before any of this, when you first heard "AI" tied to a business solution, what went through your head?

Dimitrios:

I'm a tech guy, I like technology. I thought, this is going to change the game. I didn't know exactly how yet, but I knew it would be a game changer for business owners and operators. I think we're just scratching the surface. You guys are doing a great job, but there's so much untapped potential, at least in the food industry, to help business owners maximize profits and provide the best service.

OpenCX:

Now that it's actually running, what can Nexx Burger do today that it genuinely couldn't do before?

Dimitrios:

Specifically for our catering truck, it's a game changer. Our food minimum for the truck is $1,750, so missing a call like that and losing it to a competitor really hurts the brand. Right now it's optimized my phone presence while improving in-store service. And there's a positive trend beyond that too, everyone feels more relaxed. When you're really busy and the phone keeps ringing, you're running back and forth between the register and the phone. That relieves a lot of pressure for my team, and I think they work better under less pressure. It translates down to the customer.

OpenCX:

With options out there ranging from a general AI assistant to other enterprise platforms, what tipped it toward OpenCX for you?

Dimitrios:

Your team is phenomenal. They really understood my vision, and even took it further, giving me ideas on how to make it better. They weren't shoving the product down my throat, it was more like, "here's what we could do for you, take some time, think about it." Raul did a great job explaining what the company could do. Once I signed up and started working with the engineers, it felt very family-friendly, and Nexx Burger is a family-owned business, so I really felt that culture. Everything you guys say you could do, you honor, and you go above and beyond. And the platform is so user-friendly, it's like an iPhone. You could give it to someone who's 70 or someone who's 10, and they'd both know how to use it.

OpenCX:

Beyond recovering revenue, how does this actually change the edge you have against other places in the industry?

Dimitrios:

I see competitors where the phone rings and rings and eventually stops, that's potential revenue lost. First, I'm not losing that revenue anymore. On top of that, when you call us, the AI can educate you about our product, sometimes even better than a new hire would. A new trainee has a learning curve, the AI already knows the answers. So if a customer asks something important, we have the knowledge to back it up, and we're not wasting time grabbing a manager. It's also a load off my team. When you're relaxed instead of stressed, that comes through to the customer, you can smile, and they feel it.

OpenCX:

Last question, where do you see this going for Nexx Burger from here?

Dimitrios:

I think the next step is potentially integrating AI into our drive-thru. That doesn't mean cutting staff, it means pushing our people more toward better front-of-house service. I'm all about service, the product has to be good and the service has to be good. I definitely see the company growing with OpenCX, a long-term partnership. We're a family-owned business planning to expand, my goal is 25 stores, and I really think working with OpenCX is going to help me bring in more revenue and keep my team relaxed and happy.
